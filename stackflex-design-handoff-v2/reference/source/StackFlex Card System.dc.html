4Recommendation and tokens
4aRecommendation

Colors: B, tool-based. A tool has one color everywhere, and the stack still reads as a colorful collection. Category colors (A) repeat what the label already says and make Cursor and Claude Code look alike. Pearl (C) is the most restrained and the safest alternative, but it loses the color you liked in the cards.

Main: a material, not a size. The holo foil goes over whatever tool is first, on top of its own color, plus a small blue ✦ badge on the coin. Tool color and Main never mix: change the first pick and only the foil moves.

Label: centered header. It spans the full card width, so even AUTOMATE fits at 88px, and it never meets the Main badge. The top-left pill (2a) also works if you want something closer to today.

Scaling. One component, sized from its width. Layouts (panel, social card, banners) pick a card size of at least 88px at real viewing size and change the composition when space runs out. Nothing is hidden. Below 88px the chip variant takes over, and it still shows the category.
