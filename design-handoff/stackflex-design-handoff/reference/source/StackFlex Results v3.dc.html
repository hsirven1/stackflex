Critique of the current screen. The page preview has four frames around it (section card, gray well, browser window, page). Its labels repeat: "Your Stack Page", "Collection", "My AI Stack". The full encoded URL is shown twice and is the widest element on the screen. The Banner, Post, Bio and Copy stack sections are separate widgets of equal weight, so the Social Post ends up below the fold. And there are six black buttons competing with each other.
Two concepts. 1a, Editorial studio: the page stays on the left and a tabbed "Share elsewhere" column sits on the right. 1b, Focused workspace: a single large stage, with a rail to switch between Page, Banner, Post and Bio. 1b is the calmest, but it hides the page as soon as you open another format, and it shows only one output per screen.
Recommendation: 1a. The page is always on screen as the hero, and one real-looking share format is visible next to it without scrolling. The tabs carry a small drawing of each format's shape, so it's clear what you're switching to. Only Copy link uses the ink and lime treatment; everything else is quiet. Copy stack is merged into the Bio tab (Headline/About), and the Portfolio banner is left out of this screen.

Hierarchy

Level 1, your page. Left column, about 60%. One canvas: a 34px browser bar and the page itself. No outer card, no gray well, no "Collection" or "My AI Stack" heading. The avatar, name and title carry the context.

Level 2, share elsewhere. Right column, 520px at 1440. Three tabs (Banner, Post, Bio), each with a tiny drawing of its shape. Only one preview is shown at a time, and its controls sit directly under it.

Header. The wordmark, plus Edit stack and Start over. The row of stack icons is removed: the page already shows the stack.

Actions

Link field. A 46px read-only field (#FAF9F6, 1px #E6E3DC inset, radius 13). The domain is in ink, followed by the middle-truncated path. Copy link (ink, lime coin) sits inside on the right; it is the only ink+lime button. On click it turns lime and reads "Copied" for 2s. The full real URL is copied.

Preview ↗ is a 46px outlined button next to the field. It opens the public page in a new tab.

Edit profile is a small chip in the top-right corner of the page canvas, next to what it edits (photo, name, title).

Images: Download (ink, small) and Copy image (text). The LinkedIn/X and Light/Dark switches sit above them. "Choose tools" opens the existing picker for featured tools.

Bio: Headline/About switch, a Style dropdown (Minimal, With emojis, Bold), the title field, a LinkedIn-style preview, and Copy for LinkedIn.

Sizing and spacing

Page cards: 104px at 1440 and 96px at 1280 and on mobile. Gap is 10px (about 9.6% of the card). Maximum 5 per row on desktop, 3 on mobile. Incomplete rows are centered. Cards never grow to fill the space.

Page canvas: radius 20, a 1px #ECEAE4 ring, a soft shadow and two faint glows. Avatar 84 (72 on laptop, 64 on mobile), name in Display 36/32/28.

Columns: page 1fr and share 520 (460 at 1280), gap 56, separated by a 1px #EEECE6 hairline. Side padding 56 (40 at 1280).

Previews: the exports are rendered at their design size and scaled down (banner 920→W, post 400→360). Their cards are therefore identical to the export.

Mobile: one column. Page, then Copy link (full width, 48px tall), then Open and Edit profile, a divider, and Share elsewhere. Nothing is sticky.
